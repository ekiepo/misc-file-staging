Introducing Morpheus

BRASS INTEGRATED 2700K & 3000K UPLIGHT/DOWNLIGHT

Morpheus transforms landscape lighting from something static into something that can adapt, move, and evolve.

Morpheus is a first-of-its-kind smart landscape lighting fixture that gives lighting professionals unprecedented control over the shape, direction, and intensity of light—from the Dauer Smart App. At its core is a patent-pending liquid crystal lens system that electronically reshapes the beam without motors, moving optics, interchangeable lenses, or manual adjustments to the fixture. With Dauer’s proprietary Kinetic Lighting Effect—or KLE—one or multiple lighting positions can be connected to create gradual movement through the landscape.

Save scenes and presets to the cloud, schedule lighting around sunrise and sunset, manage multiple users, and make adjustments without repeated trips into the landscape.
